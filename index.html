02 / ACADEMIC PROJECT
Jul 2021–Apr 2022 · CATIA V5 / SolidWorks / ANSYS

Automated gear inspection system

Designed a mechanical system concept to help identify defect patterns in manufactured gears. Modelled mechanical components, performed structural/load calculations based on engineering specifications and conducted FEM analysis in ANSYS Workbench.

CAD modellingLoad calculationsANSYS WorkbenchFEM

Documented design calculations: belt speed 0.01571 m/s; calculated belt pull 10.755 N; estimated conveyor drive power 0.1689 W; frame bending stress 14.06 N/mm² under the report's stated assumptions.

Engineering method & limitations
  • Problem: inspect high-volume manufactured gears for visible defects.
  • Concept: camera-based image acquisition, conveyor, supporting frame and separator for defective gears.
  • Report calculations cover conveyor speed, belt pull, drive power, belt length, frame bending and separator torque.
  • FEA boundary conditions documented: fixed support, 1 kg object load (9.81 N) and standard gravity (9.81 m/s²).
  • CAD design is documented; the report does not provide verified inspection accuracy or a clear completed test result.

The report contains assumptions and some inconsistencies that should be reviewed before using the figures as validated design results. Do not claim a validated prototype or inspection performance from this report alone.

HOW I WORK
Repeatable engineering process

From requirement to validation

A good engineering decision is traceable. I aim to show not only the final model or contour plot, but also the inputs and assumptions behind it.

RequirementsCADCalculationsValidation
Checklist
  • What must the design do?
  • Which loads, dimensions and material properties govern it?
  • What simplified calculation checks the order of magnitude?
  • Are CAD constraints and interfaces represented correctly?
  • Does simulation/test evidence meet a defined criterion?
  • What are the limitations?
03 / First principles

Show the reasoning, not just the result.

For a component-level structural check, begin with a free-body diagram, establish equilibrium, identify the load path, and estimate the dominant stress or deflection before relying on FEA. Then compare the simplified model with the simulation setup and document where the assumptions differ.

For vehicle mass properties, use consistent coordinates and verified component data. Check the effect of configuration changes rather than relying on a single headline number.

What every calculation should contain

  • Known inputs and their source
  • Assumptions and units
  • Governing equation
  • Substitution and result
  • Engineering interpretation
  • Limitations / validation plan
Integrity note: any generic example must be labelled illustrative. Do not present illustrative numbers as MAN, Tesla or project results.
